Job Application Project

I. Problems

	A. Applicants
	
		1. Can't get jobs
		
		2. Receive no feedback from employers
		
			a. employers never respond
			
			b. employers never explain why they weren't chosen
			
		3. Time waste
		
		4. Vague job requirements
		
		5. Guessing salary
		
		6. No communication with real humans
		
		7. fake jobs
		
		8. applicant tracking systems (ATS)
		
			a. AI processes resumes
	
	B. Employers
	
		1. too many applicants to chose from
		
		2. can't find the right talent
		
II. Application Solutions

	A. Required communication
	
		1. employers MUST respond to every applicant they receive with a clear reason as to why the applicant was not chosen
	
	B. Limited applications
	
		1. Applicants can only apply for a fixed amount of jobs
		
		2. Employers can only receive a fixed amount of applicants for a given job. Applicants must be addressed and rejected if they want to receive more.
	
	C. Employer reputation score
	
		1. Applicants can rate employers according to real metrics
		
	D. Metrics
	
		1. Employers log qualifications for jobs. They reject candidates based on these qualifications.
